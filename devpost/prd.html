2. Sketch Health Bar

Chalk sketch aesthetic featuring an outlined container with textured diagonal hatching:

  • • Safe (>60%): Dense, steady diagonal hatch lines
  • • Cautious (20–60%): Sparser, lighter hatching with open space
  • • Danger (<20%): Erratic, fragmented sketch strokes
  • • GSAP: Dynamic line-drawing & morphing transitions

3. Typography Hierarchy

High-character pairing for an analog, human feel:

  • • "Adios" Serif: Distinctive headline & brand display serif for calm warmth
  • • "FORUM" Sans: Spaced, clean all-caps sans-serif for labels, badges, and numbers
  • • No generic UI look: Intentional craft that avoids sterile corporate fintech styles
